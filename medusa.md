Hello — you probably just scanned this at BostonFIG.

**Medusa's Garden** is a board game by Phil Gross and Jono Naito-Tetro, published by DVC Games. It is already out, and it is [on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/456133/medusas-garden). This app is the phone version of it that I built for them: your phone's front-facing camera becomes the mirror, and the game is played by passing it around the table.

### Try it

**On iPhone or iPad** — [install the beta through TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/c3nXFFU2). You will need Apple's free TestFlight app; the link walks you through it.

**On Android** — not yet, sorry. Android testing is invite-only at the moment, so there is no link I can put here. Email <help@tripleli.com> and I will add you as soon as there is a build to add you to.

Either way, I would genuinely like to hear what you thought — the same address works for that.

### I build these

Porting a board game to a companion app is the work I am looking for more of, and this is the best example of it I have. If you have a game that wants one, come find me at the table or write to <help@tripleli.com>.
